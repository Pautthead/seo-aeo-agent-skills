## Mandatory caveats

**Information gain is not a confirmed Google requirement.** It is modelled from US11354342B2 (answer composition) and an Ahrefs analysis (2026-08-20). Google has not confirmed any information gain requirement. There is active practitioner dissent ([r/SEO, 2026-01-26](https://www.reddit.com/r/SEO/comments/1qngfmm/is_programmatic_seo_still_a_good_move_in_2026/)).

**This score is judged by meaning.** Fact enumeration, baseline matching, and dimension arithmetic are deterministic. Whether a number is informative or is padding is a judgement about meaning and is not reproducible. Treat the score as a structured opinion, not a measurement.

**This is not a citation or traffic forecast.** No study links this score to citation rate. GEO vendor data shows AI visibility can rise while organic traffic falls 66% (Lily Ray). Authority gets sites into the indexes feeding AI products; correlation is not causation.

**Review does not create the original fact.** Lily Ray, *The AI Slop Loop*, 2026-04-14: a human forwarded wrong information and the artefact still arrived wrong. Review catches errors. It does not manufacture the missing fact. Google ran four spam updates in 2026 versus one in 2025; Gary Illyes (Search Central Live Barcelona, 2026-10) says scaled AI content is now a bigger problem than link spam.

**Coverage is a sample.** Full mode reads 3–5 pages. Lite mode reads 3 and stops the ledger at 25 rows. Neither is the source set an AI answer was synthesized from. A lite score is not comparable to a full score. No reliable public source gives a citation-lift window, so do not quote one.
